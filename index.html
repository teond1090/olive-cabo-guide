About this tab

for the curious

A phone browser can't tune a real radio — there's no hardware access for it. So Olive does two things instead. Live looks up the broadcasters actually licensed around you, wherever in the country you are, and shows the dial position to turn to — many of them you can also stream right here. The August route is Olive's own hand-checked list for the trip, and it works with no signal at all.
