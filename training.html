It builds a narrated tour of the app, about three minutes long — every tab, what it's for, and the handful of things worth doing before you leave. Press Create the video and it renders here, then saves to your downloads as an MP4 you can text to anyone.

Before you leave — on every phone

  1. Open teond1090.github.io/olive-cabo-guide and add it to the home screen.
  2. Allow location when asked. Nearly everything depends on it.
  3. Tap the 🔊 button once so the phone lets Clara speak.
  4. Toolkit → Save for offline now, then Also save her narrations. Do this on wifi.

That last one matters most. Havre has no internet and half of Montana has no signal.
